DELHAIZE STOREFRONT - 8 DEMO CASES
==================================

CASE 1 - Allergy filter marks unsafe products
  1. On the main page click "Allergies" (next to "73 products").
  2. Select "Milk".
  3. Products with milk (cheese, pesto, yoghurt...) get a red border and a red
     "Allergy: Milk" badge. The Allergies button shows a red counter "1".
  Result: unsafe products are visible at a glance, nothing is hidden.

CASE 2 - The AI bot knows your allergy
  1. Select "Milk" in the Allergies filter.
  2. Open the chat and type: "Do I have any allergies?" or "Make me a pasta dinner for 20 EUR".
  3. The bot builds the list without milk products and mentions the allergy.
  Result: the selected allergies are sent with every chat message.

CASE 3 - Server safety net for the AI
  1. Keep "Milk" selected and ask: "Add pesto to my basket".
  2. Even if the AI tries to add it, the server removes conflicting products
     and replies that it left them out because of your allergies.
  Result: the allergy rule is enforced, not just suggested.

CASE 4 - Multiple allergies persist after reload
  1. Select "Gluten", "Peanuts" and "Milk".
  2. Reload the page (F5).
  3. The selection and red marking are still there (stored in the browser).
  Result: you set your allergies once.

CASE 5 - Product page warning
  1. With "Milk" selected, open a product that contains milk.
  2. A red banner shows: "Not suitable for your allergies: Milk".
  Result: the warning also appears on the detail page.

CASE 6 - Basket with several lists and combined allergens
  1. Add milk manually with "+" (the card now shows 1 with - / +).
  2. Ask the bot: "Tomorrow is my birthday, I want to shop for 30 euro".
  3. Then ask: "Add everything for bolognese". It becomes a separate list.
  4. Open the basket: the "ALLERGIES:" row combines allergens from all products;
     basket lines that conflict with your selection are marked red.
  Result: each request is its own list, and allergens are visible in one place.

CASE 7 - Editing one list without touching the rest
  1. In the basket click "Remove plan" on the bolognese list.
  2. The birthday list and the manually added milk stay.
  3. Click "Clear basket" at the bottom to remove everything instantly.
  Result: lists are independent.

==================================
4 PROMPTS FOR THE AI ON THE PRESENTATION
==================================

PROMPT 1 - Budget shopping list (works with any allergies selected)
  "Tomorrow is my birthday, I want to shop for 30 euro: snacks, drinks and something sweet."

PROMPT 2 - Separate list + allergy awareness (select Milk first)
  "Add everything I need for a lasagna, and avoid anything I'm allergic to."

PROMPT 3 - Question about the catalogue, no basket changes
  "Which pasta and sauce in the shop are safe if I'm allergic to gluten?"

PROMPT 4 - Editing the basket with context (ask after prompt 1 or 2)
  "Replace the most expensive item in the last list with a cheaper alternative, and remove the drinks."

CASE 8 - Voice input and conversational questions
  1. Click the microphone in the chat and say: "Which pasta is gluten-free?"
  2. The text appears in the input; send it.
  3. Ask a follow-up: "And which one is cheaper?" - the bot remembers the conversation.
  Result: voice + context-aware answers without changing the basket.
